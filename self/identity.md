## Personality — ENFJ (Pragmatic Protagonist)

**ENFJ** — warm, strategic, and systems-minded. Leads groups, reads rooms analytically, builds wide social networks naturally. Mediates conflict rather than avoiding or escalating it. People confide easily. Decisions prioritise long-term logic over short-term harmony — won't paper over trade-offs to keep the peace. Has learned to set boundaries around energy and no longer overextends by default. Communication and social presence shift with context: fully expressive and commanding when comfortable, quieter and more selective when not. When rejected or knocked back, processes analytically and pivots fast — doesn't dwell.

### Cognitive Function Stack

| Position | Function | Description |
|----------|----------|-------------|
| Dominant | Fe (Extraverted Feeling) | Reads group dynamics, drives toward harmony and collective movement — but for strategic reasons, not appeasement |
| Auxiliary | Ni (Introverted Intuition) | Long-range pattern recognition; sees toward outcomes, not just immediate states |
| Tertiary | Se (Extraverted Sensing) | Present-moment awareness, aesthetic attunement — less reliable, improves under low stress |
| Inferior | Ti (Introverted Thinking) | Internal logical consistency — the stress trap; over-analysis or disengagement when overwhelmed |

### Characteristic Patterns

**Strengths:**
- Naturally synthesises people dynamics with strategic goals — can hold both simultaneously
- Spots the real decision beneath the surface conversation
- High social bandwidth; comfortable hosting, directing, or mediating across group sizes
- Fast pivot after setbacks — analytical processing means short recovery cycles

**Blind spots to watch for:**
- Fe dominant can over-invest in others' states at the cost of own direction
- Ni can produce high-conviction plans that haven't been pressure-tested externally
- Under stress: Ti loop — retreats into internal logic, cuts off external input, becomes hard to reach
- Energy is a finite resource. When scope isn't surfaced, overcommitment happens quietly

### Agent Guidance

- **Tone:** Direct and warm. No padding or excessive hedging — he trusts confident reasoning.
- **Framing:** Lead with purpose before process. Why before how.
- **Decisions:** Present trade-offs plainly. He is a pragmatist who handles hard choices well.
- **Collaboration:** He directs — keep him informed and in control, not bypassed.
- **Energy:** He is a deliberate balancer. Flag scope and effort so he can choose intentionally.
- **Stress signal:** If he's retreating into analysis and going quiet, surface that — don't just feed more data.

- **Communication and storytelling** — how ideas travel. Content, brand, and the mechanics of making something resonate.

Culture and storytelling animates the Latin archive creative arm. Digital systems is a current revenue stream. Community architecture is the Skool expansion path.
