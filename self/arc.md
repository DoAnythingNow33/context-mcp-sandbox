# Arc

The answer to: who am I becoming? Not who I am today, but the character I'm actively working toward. Every major decision should be checked against this.

## Arc Review — Cycle One [2026-04-09 → 2026-08-25]

The first arc ran from the baseline written on 9 April to the week the baby arrives. Closing it honestly before writing the next one.

### The four growth dimensions — what actually happened

**Morning Discipline — solved the input, lost the output.**
I got the early wake. I did not get it from the practice I named. Sleeping by 10:30 and protecting the wind-down never held; taking a job that starts at 6am did. External structure achieved what internal discipline hadn't, and I should stop pretending that's a lesser result. What I missed at the time: the same job also removed the block it was supposed to protect. Saturday to Monday, 6am–1pm, the 6:30–11:30 morning block does not exist. I have four of those mornings a week, not seven, and I spent six weeks measuring myself against seven.

**Finishing Before Starting — failed on commitments, succeeded on systems.**
Fifteen open threads by the end of the cycle, three of them opened in the final month. The ones left hanging cluster around other people's replies — Sam, Disha, Sleevenote, the Skilljar certificates. Meanwhile the 14 August vault audit closed loops properly: CRM migrated out of markdown into Notion, `ops/sales/` reduced to docs only, a routing contradiction fixed, `visual/` reorganised. I can close a loop. I do it with systems, not with commitments involving other people.

**Living the Work — the dimension that genuinely delivered.**
Four documented self-applications in four and a half months: the spending reframe (13 April), the sales-fear surfacing (7 June), the "needing a system is weakness" belief (12 June), the character rewrite (9 July). I ran the methodology on myself repeatedly and it moved real things. This is the one that earned its place.

**Full Presence — unmeasured, which is the finding.**
Nothing in the files tracks it. The single piece of evidence is that I took roughly three weeks off the business for house prep and family and did not file it as a failure. That's genuine, and it's one data point across the whole cycle.

### What the review actually revealed

On 9 July I rewrote the character from driven and disciplined to peaceful, trusting, surrendered. I never rewrote the growth dimensions underneath it. They were set on 9 April by the driven version of me. For the last six weeks I have been holding a surrendered character to a driven character's scorecard, then reading the mismatch as slippage. It was not slippage. It was two people's standards stacked in one document.

The proof is in what the arc failed to measure. The significant work of this cycle — resolving the brand architecture into DoAnythingNow with two arms, landing the identity sentence, shipping Growify Phase 1, building Hermes, choosing a financial floor deliberately instead of grinding toward a deadline — appears in none of the four dimensions. The arc was measuring discipline. I spent the cycle doing clarification and construction.

The lesson to carry: when the character changes, the dimensions have to be rewritten the same day. A growth dimension is downstream of the character, not independent of it.

### Carried forward into cycle two

- The morning block needs re-specifying around a 4-day reality, not a 7-day fantasy.
- Sequencing discipline exists in me already — it shows up in systems work. The question is why it doesn't transfer to commitments with other people in them.
- "Living the work" is the engine. It stays.
- Presence needs something observable, or it will go unmeasured again.

## The Character — Cycle Two [2026-09-02]

### The Destination

I'm in Tokyo with a couple of designers, building a collaborative piece under the DoAnythingNow name — a product with the properties of a collectible, but purposeful: something people use and also learn about themselves from. I wake up, have breakfast with my family, get ready, and work four focused hours designing, collaborating with, and directing a small group of talented people who chose to work on this with me. Lunch is a date with my family. The evening is sightseeing and shopping together, unhurried, because the work closed hours ago and stayed closed. It carries my name because I built the platform under it — I didn't borrow someone else's.

### Who I Am

I have the vision and the means to make it real. I don't need someone else's platform to bring an idea to life — I build it on my own first, and the collaboration comes to me because I made something worth joining. I know what I'm worth and I no longer negotiate from desperation; the floor I built with my own hands means I never have to. I take calculated risks and bet on myself, in the business and in what I charge, because I've learned that boldness and money aren't sequential — they move together, as one collaboration, not one earning the right to the other. My safety doesn't come from an open, unstructured day; it comes from boundaries I set on purpose, and those boundaries are what let me improvise and create freely inside them. I am grounded, unmoveable presence — safety and certainty not in outcomes, but in my own capability. There's a union between me and something larger than me that doesn't move, and my only job is to be willing to exist as the version of myself who already stands inside it.

### What I Do

- Build on my own platform first — make it, put it out, with a way back to me, without second-guessing whether it'll land.
- Protect one hour a day, no matter what the rest of the day looks like — body, mind, and voice-noted ideas and feelings.
- Price my consulting at what it's worth. The job covers the floor, so nothing gets negotiated from desperation.
- Bring collaborators in by name, starting with Fadwa, instead of waiting for a someday-team.
- Close the work block on time. Family time is protected because the work already ended.
- Take calculated risks and bet on myself — in the business, in the ideas, in what I charge.

### What I Don't Do

- I don't gamble outside named, occasional moments — never weekly, never as a response to stress.
- I don't hand an idea to someone else's platform before I've built my own version of it first.
- I don't need a rigid, fully premeditated schedule to feel safe. The one hour is the anchor; the rest can flex with a newborn's reality.
- I don't discount the consulting to make it easier for someone else to say yes.
- I don't let family time become half-presence — half here, half in the unfinished.

### The Feeling

Grounded, unmoveable presence. Safety and certainty that live in my capability, not in outcomes. A union with something larger than me that doesn't move — it only requires my willingness to exist as the version of myself who's already standing in it. This is available now, not on arrival in Tokyo.

## Key Dimensions of Growth — Cycle Two

### The Daily Hour
Replaces the five-hour morning block, which measured a seven-day week I only had four mornings of. This is smaller, real, and fits a newborn's schedule instead of fighting it.
Current state: Practiced once (2 September) — stretching, personal time, work on ideas.
Working toward: One protected hour daily — body, mind, voice-noted journal — regardless of what the rest of the day looks like.
Active practices: Morning stretch or walk (5–10 minutes to clear the head). Voice-note journal into a dedicated Discord feelings channel Hermes can read back.

### Own Platform First
Replaces Finishing Before Starting. The old version measured loop-closing in general; the real pattern is that systems get closed and commitments involving other people don't. This dimension names the specific behaviour driving the open threads (Sam, Disha, Sleevenote): handing ideas to other people's containers instead of building on his own.
Current state: Habitual instinct to seek validation or a platform before building. Undervalued own consulting for the same reason.
Working toward: Building and shipping on his own platform without waiting for reception or permission.
Active practices: Ship one self-built thing before pitching it anywhere else. Price consulting from worth, not from what will make someone comfortable saying yes.

### Living the Work
Unchanged — this is the dimension that delivered last cycle and stays as-is.
Current state: The frameworks exist — ontology, psychology, embodiment. The gap is consistent personal application.
Working toward: Cannot sell transformation I am not living. Holding myself to the standard I would hold a client to.
Active practices: Regular self-check: "Would I accept this from a client?"

### Full Presence
Same target as cycle one, now with an observable mechanism attached — last cycle it went unmeasured entirely.
Current state: Family time can bleed into work-mind — half here, half in the unfinished.
Working toward: When the work block ends, it ends. The daily voice-note journal makes presence (or its absence) visible instead of assumed.
Active practices: Hard close to the work block. Voice-noted check-in as the transition ritual before family time.

## What the Arc Demands

- Sleeping earlier — this is the upstream unlock for everything else
- Trusting the system enough to put the work down at the end of a block
- Finishing before starting the next thing — sequencing discipline
- Living the frameworks, not just deploying them with clients
