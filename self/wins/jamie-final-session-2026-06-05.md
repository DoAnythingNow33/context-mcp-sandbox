# Win: Jamie Final Session — AI Content Engine Delivered

## What Was Built

**AI Pulse Report** — a scheduled agent that researches daily news and trends relevant to Jamie's industry and lands a formatted briefing directly into his Gmail drafts every morning. No extra step — it's there when he opens email.

**Content Engine** — pulls from the Pulse Report to surface relevant talking points for Jamie's company social. Report goes in, LinkedIn and Twitter angles come out. Reduces the blank-page problem to near zero.

**Obsidian Vault + Base** — a content bank set up to track, draft, and manage LinkedIn and Twitter content. The Base gives him a structured view across all content in progress and published.

## Why This Matters

This is a complete, working AI workflow delivered to a real client — not a demo, not a prototype. The full loop: research → synthesis → content → scheduling. Jamie left with something that runs without him having to think about it.
