# self/ — Identity & Personal Foundation

## What this workspace is for

This is the identity layer. Files here are profile-based — updated periodically (quarterly or when something genuinely shifts), not daily. The arc is the north star. Everything in entities/, calendar/, and ops/content/ serves it.

## Files

| File | What it is | When to read |
|---|---|---|
| `goals.md` | Active threads — business targets, family runway, open projects | Every session |
| `arc.md` | Identity evolution — who you're becoming, growth dimensions | Coaching / self-reflection sessions |
| `writing-dna.md` | Voice and structural patterns for all content | Any content creation session |
| `beliefs.md` | Core values and working principles | When facing a decision that feels off |
| `identity.md` | Cognitive style (ENFJ) + domains of genuine curiosity — merged profile | When calibrating tone, approach, or connecting themes |

## When to update

- `goals.md` — after /weekly-review or whenever a thread opens or closes
- `arc.md` — when a growth dimension moves (not more than monthly)
- `beliefs.md` — when a conviction crystallises or shifts
- `writing-dna.md` — when voice evolves; extract from new content that lands well

## Skills that apply here

None load directly into self/ — but this workspace feeds:
- `/weekly-review` reads `goals.md`
- `/wrap` writes back to `goals.md` when threads shift
- `/linkedin-engine` always loads `writing-dna.md`
