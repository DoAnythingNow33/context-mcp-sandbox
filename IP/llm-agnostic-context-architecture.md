# LLM-Agnostic Context Architecture

## What It Is

Structuring all company or personal knowledge in a format that is completely independent of any AI model or provider. The context layer is yours; the AI is a utility that plugs into it, not a platform you build on top of.

## Why It Matters

Most AI implementations are invisible platform bets — workflows built inside a tool, proprietary formats, trust in a company's pricing, terms, and longevity. When that changes, everything breaks. LLM-Agnostic Context Architecture decouples your knowledge from any single provider. When a better model drops, you switch. When a company changes terms, you leave. The context stays. This isn't just a technical choice — it's a strategic asset vs. a perpetual dependency.

## How to Use It

**As a coach:** When a client asks "what if I pick the wrong AI?" — this is the answer. Their folder works with any model. The AI changes; their context doesn't. The work they put into structuring their knowledge is permanent.

**As a consultant:** Use this framing to justify the folder-first deliverable to stakeholders who are nervous about vendor lock-in. "You're not buying into Claude. You're building your context layer. Any AI can run it." Positions the engagement as building a durable asset, not a tool dependency.

**As a content creator:** The "you're vendor-locked and you don't know it" post. What happens to your workflows when OpenAI changes pricing, deprecates a model, or gets acquired? The answer is a folder on your machine that any AI can read.

## Content Angles

- Everyone talks about picking the right AI. Nobody talks about making sure you can switch.
- "We use Claude" is a product decision. "We have a context layer" is a strategic asset.
- You don't own your AI stack until your context lives on your machine — not in a chat thread, not in a SaaS tool.
- What happens to five years of accumulated context when the AI company changes its terms?
