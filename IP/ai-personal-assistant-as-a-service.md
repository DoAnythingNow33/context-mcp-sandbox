# AI Personal Assistant as a Service

## What It Is

A productised consulting offer: set up a founder's context folder and always-on AI agent, charged as a setup fee plus monthly retainer. Positioned against hiring a human personal assistant — a fraction of the annual cost, no social boundaries, available at 2am, and it adds a layer between the founder and any human assistant rather than replacing one.

## Why It Matters

Most founders either don't have a personal assistant or feel they can't justify one. This offer makes the decision obvious: comparable capability at a fraction of the cost, delivered in days not weeks. The first client delivery creates the onboarding playbook that makes the offer repeatable and scalable. It's also the most concrete, relatable framing for what AI consulting actually produces — not a vague "transformation," but a personal assistant that works 24/7.

## How to Use It

**As a coach:** Use the comparison to reframe AI adoption for clients who are stuck on cost. A human EA costs £30–50k/year and has social limits. This costs £2–5k setup and a few hundred a month, and has no limits on when or what you ask.

**As a consultant:** This is a lead offer — concrete, fast to deliver, immediately valuable. It opens the door to the larger context architecture engagement. Proof-of-concept delivery to one client (Growify) creates the playbook; the playbook makes the offer scalable.

**As a content creator:** The value-framing angle: founders talk about needing an EA and not being able to afford one. This post reframes what they already have access to and makes the gap obvious.
