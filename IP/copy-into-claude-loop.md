# Copy-Into-Claude Loop

## What It Is

A troubleshooting reflex for non-technical users navigating AI setup: when you hit a wall, paste the error or screen into Claude and follow the next instruction. Repeat until it works. No diagnosis required, no understanding needed in advance.

## Why It Matters

Most people assume you need to understand a system before you can operate it. The Copy-Into-Claude Loop inverts this — you build competence through forward motion, not prior knowledge. The loop creates a feedback cycle where confidence accumulates from successful completion of hard things, not from understanding how they work. This is how non-technical people actually get fluent with AI tooling: not by learning, by looping.

## How to Use It

**As a coach:** Teach the reflex explicitly in session 1. Name it. When a client hits friction, say "what does the loop tell you to do?" They learn to self-rescue rather than wait for you. The goal is for the loop to become their default before they reach out.

**As a consultant:** Builds into onboarding protocol for AI adoption — give non-technical staff the loop as a first principle before any specific tooling. Reduces support load immediately. Also useful as a diagnostic: clients who won't loop are usually blocked by something other than technical friction (trust, permission, fear of breaking things).

**As a content creator:** This is the real answer to "I'm not technical enough for AI." The post almost writes itself: most people think the barrier is knowledge — it's actually just a missing reflex.
