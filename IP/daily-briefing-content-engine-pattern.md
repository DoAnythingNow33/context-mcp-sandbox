# The Daily Briefing → Content Engine Pattern

## What It Is

A two-stage automated workflow that converts daily research into ready-to-use content angles.

**Stage 1 — The Briefing:** A scheduled agent runs on a defined cadence (daily/weekly), researches a topic domain relevant to the client's industry, and delivers a formatted briefing to an inbox (Gmail drafts, Notion, Slack). No manual trigger needed.

**Stage 2 — The Content Engine:** The briefing becomes raw material for a second pass — a Claude prompt that extracts talking points, angles, or hooks tailored to the client's voice and platform (LinkedIn, Twitter, newsletters).

## Why It Works

Removes two of the biggest friction points in content creation: *what to talk about* and *where to start*. The blank page problem becomes nearly zero. The client's job shrinks to editing and posting.

The briefing also has standalone value — it keeps the client informed without research time. Content is a byproduct of staying current.

## Replication Notes

The architecture is the same regardless of industry. Variables to swap:
- Research domain (industry, competitors, keywords)
- Briefing format (bullets, narrative, structured by section)
- Platform voice (LinkedIn vs Twitter vs newsletter)
- Delivery destination (Gmail drafts, Notion, Slack)

## Content / Sales Use

Lead with the outcome: "Every morning, your Gmail has a draft. You read it, you edit it, you post it. That's the whole workflow."

The setup is one session. The value compounds daily. Price accordingly.
