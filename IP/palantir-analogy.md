# The Palantir Analogy

## What It Is

Palantir Technologies spends years building ontologies for defense and enterprise clients at costs in the millions. Their product is structured intelligence — a single source of context that lets decision-makers see across an entire system. The analogy: what Palantir does for governments and large enterprises at enormous expense, the Folder-First approach makes available to a 10-person agency or a solo founder.

## Why It Matters

Most people have no reference point for what an "ontology" is or why it's valuable. Palantir gives them one. It anchors the concept to something proven, prestigious, and expensive — which repositions the work from "AI consulting" to "strategic intelligence infrastructure." The scale difference (millions vs. thousands) becomes a proof of value rather than a barrier.

## How to Use It

**As a coach:** Less applicable here — Palantir is a business framing, not a personal one.

**As a consultant:** Use it in the initial pitch or discovery call to establish the category. "What Palantir builds for defense contractors at millions of dollars — I build a version of that for businesses like yours." Then show what it looks like in practice immediately (live folder, real example).

**As a content creator:** The post is about access. What was once locked behind enterprise contracts and seven-figure budgets is now available at the scale of a laptop and a folder. That's a genuinely interesting story.

## Content Angles

- The most valuable thing Palantir sells isn't software. It's structured context. And that's no longer exclusive.
- Defense contractors pay millions for what's now available to any business willing to build it properly.
- If Palantir built something for your business, what would it cost? Now imagine 1/1000th of that price and 10x more relevance to what you actually do.
