# Discrepancy Detection

## What It Is

When an AI system holds both a person or organisation's stated vision and their actual daily activity in the same context folder, it automatically surfaces contradictions — gaps between what they say they're building and what they're actually doing.

## Why It Matters

Most strategy failures aren't execution failures — they're perception failures. People believe they're working toward a goal while their actual time, attention, and energy patterns tell a different story. Discrepancy Detection makes this visible without accusation. The AI isn't judging — it's pattern-matching. That gives the coach or consultant a neutral surface to work from.

Live example: a coaching client who stated a £5k/month revenue goal while maintaining an £80/month client relationship. The AI flagged it without being asked. That one observation reframed the whole session.

## How to Use It

**As a coach:** "Let's look at what your context file actually shows versus what you told me your priorities are." The contradiction is the session. You don't have to find it — the system surfaces it.

**As a consultant:** In the ontology build process, discrepancy detection is the first diagnostic tool. After extracting each person's workflows, run the vision against the reality. The gaps are the consulting mandate.

**As a content creator:** AI doesn't just answer questions. It catches contradictions. That's a different value proposition than most people understand — and a post that cuts through.
