# Folder-First Philosophy

## What It Is

Everything that defines a person, role, or business lives in a local folder they own — not inside an AI tool, not inside a SaaS platform. The `CLAUDE.md` file is the AI's ground truth for that context. AI reads only what's needed per session.

## Why It Matters

Most AI use is ephemeral — you type, it responds, nothing persists. The Folder-First Philosophy makes context cumulative and portable. The context compounds over time, belongs to the owner, and works with any AI model. Switch from Claude to OpenAI tomorrow — the folder doesn't change. This reframes AI from a smart chatbot into a persistent intelligence layer built on your own IP.

## How to Use It

**As a coach:** Each client gets their own folder. Sessions extract and update their context file. The coaching relationship becomes a growing, structured record of who they are and what's shifting — not scattered notes.

**As a consultant:** The deliverable isn't a deck or a document — it's a living folder that runs the client's context. The CLAUDE.md defines the system. Handover is trivial. Ongoing value is built into the structure, not the consultant's presence.

**As a content creator:** "Your context isn't Claude's. It isn't OpenAI's. It's yours." That's the post. The tension: most people build inside tools they don't own. The alternative: a folder on your desktop that any AI can read.

## Content Angles

- Most AI tools lock your context inside their platform. This approach locks it into a folder you own.
- The AI is just the engine. The folder is the vehicle. You own the vehicle.
- What happens to your context when the AI company changes its pricing, terms, or ceases to exist?
