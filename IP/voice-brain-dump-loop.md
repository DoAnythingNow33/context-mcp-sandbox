# Voice Brain Dump Loop

## What It Is

A weekly maintenance technique: record a short voice memo (no typing, no structure required — just talk), drop the transcript into the AI context folder, and let the AI summarise, update the relevant context files, and flag what shifted. The context stays current without manual maintenance overhead.

## Why It Matters

The biggest failure mode of any context or knowledge system is that people stop maintaining it — because maintenance feels like work. The Voice Brain Dump Loop reduces maintenance to a single low-friction input (talking) and automates the organisation. No one finds talking difficult. The discipline is in the regularity, not the effort.

Compounding effect: over time, the transcript record becomes a structured history of thinking. A four-session coaching engagement plus regular voice dumps gives the AI (and the coach) a complete picture of how the client's thinking has evolved — surface patterns they can't see themselves.

## How to Use It

**As a coach:** Each client can do weekly voice dumps between sessions. The AI processes them, updates their context file, and surfaces changes for the coach to review before the next session. The coach arrives prepared, not catching up.

**As a consultant:** Weekly voice dumps from key stakeholders replace status update meetings. The AI processes the transcript, updates the context, and flags what changed in the business.

**As a content creator:** "My productivity system fits in one voice note a week." The post is about the gap between how complicated people think maintenance needs to be and how simple it actually can be.
