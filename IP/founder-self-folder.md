# Founder Self-Folder

## What It Is

A personal context layer — beliefs, goals, identity, character arc — that lives alongside the business context folder but is kept entirely separate from it. Any AI agent, regardless of which tool is being used, can load the self-folder to know the founder as a person, not just as an operator.

## Why It Matters

Most AI setups treat the user as a role (CEO, consultant, founder) rather than a person. The self-folder fixes this: it makes the "who" portable across every AI tool, so the agent always has both the human and the business in view simultaneously. Without it, the AI knows the company but not the person running it — which produces generic, depersonalised output.

## How to Use It

**As a coach:** Use this to show clients that the system should know them — their arc, their tensions, their beliefs — not just their calendar and client list. It reframes AI adoption from productivity tool to accountability layer.

**As a consultant:** Deliver the self-folder as part of the context folder setup. It pairs with the business context folder to give the AI the full picture from session one. It's also the piece that makes the whole system feel personal rather than mechanical.

**As a content creator:** The contrast angle: everyone's building AI systems for their business, almost nobody is building one that knows who they are. The self-folder is the difference between an AI assistant and an AI that actually knows you.

## Content Angles

- Most founders set up AI to know their business. None set it up to know them. The self-folder is what changes that.
- The question people don't know to ask: "Does my AI know what I believe, or just what I do?"
- Story: a founder explains their situation to a new AI tool and has to start from scratch because there's no portable self-layer. Then contrast with the self-folder pattern.
