# Taste of Your Life

## What It Is
Extending the concept of "taste" — aesthetic discernment, the ability to recognise and curate quality — to lived experience itself. Not just what you own or consume, but the texture of your daily existence: how it feels, looks, and exists from the inside.

## Why It Matters
"Taste" is understood and respected when applied to art, food, or design. Applying it to a life makes the same discernment feel accessible and legitimate. It frames lifestyle design not as indulgence but as craft — the same attention a designer brings to a product, brought to how you exist.

## How to Use It
**As a coach:** Use it when a client is living by default — doing things because they "should" or out of inertia. Ask: "What's the taste of your life right now? What would you change if you were curating it deliberately?"

**As a consultant:** Relevant when helping clients think about the culture or working environment they want to build — the taste of an organisation is as real as the taste of a life.

**As a content creator:** A post about the difference between people with taste in things vs. taste in their actual life — and how to develop the latter.
