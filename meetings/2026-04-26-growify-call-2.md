# Growify Call 2 — 26 April 2026

**Context:** Follow-up to original proposal sent ~3 weeks prior. Goal was to walk through the proposal, clarify the vision, and agree on a starting scope.

---

## The Core Offer Explained

**Phase 1: Ontology Build**
- 4 x 2-hour one-on-one sessions (Rishabh, Disha, Chandika, Rohit/one other)
- Goal: extract how the business actually operates from the people doing the work — not from the founders' perception of it
- Output: a structured ontology of Growify as an entity — visual map (Whimsical) + deep context (Obsidian folder) showing current state, desired state, and the gap between them
- AI then runs against that context continuously to surface misalignments, over-investment, and neglected priorities

**The Palantir analogy landed:** Palantir spends years building ontologies for defense/enterprise companies at millions of dollars. This is that, smaller scale, for an agency.

---

## The Folder-First Philosophy (The actual IP)

- Everything lives in a local folder you own — not inside Claude, not inside any SaaS tool
- The `CLAUDE.md` file is the AI's ground truth for that role/context
- AI reads only what's needed per session = token efficiency
- Context is yours — swap Claude for OpenAI tomorrow, folder doesn't change
- Ownership framing: *"Your IP on your desktop"* — this resonated

**The mise en place analogy:** Sessions are prep work. Once prep is done, execution accelerates. Most AI use is the inverse.

---

## Why It's Bottom-Up (Not Top-Down)

Founders declaring "we want AI" from the top without understanding ground-level workflows produces noise. The right starting point is the people doing the daily work — extract their actual process, find where time is lost, identify what tools are missing, then build.

The discrepancy detection use case: when AI has the vision AND the current reality in the same folder, it flags contradictions automatically. Daniel's live example — his £80 coaching client vs. his stated revenue goals. AI surfaced it without being asked.

---

## The Weekly Brain Dump Loop

The method Daniel uses personally and is proposing for Growify:
1. Record a voice brain dump weekly (phone, no typing)
2. Drop transcript into the AI folder
3. AI summarises, updates context, flags what shifted
4. Context stays fresh without manual maintenance

The compounding effect: four coaching sessions with one client → AI extracted a complete methodology, content angles for every concept, a Skool curriculum.

---

## Disha's Reframe (The smart pushback)

Rather than involving all leadership, she proposed: start with just founders + process manager.
- More people = more chaos, more tool resistance, more conflicting workflows
- Founders need to define *which problems are worth solving* before going to the team
- Deploy only after you have internal results (80/20 validated before rollout)

Daniel agreed. The founders-first approach became the agreed Phase 1 scope.
