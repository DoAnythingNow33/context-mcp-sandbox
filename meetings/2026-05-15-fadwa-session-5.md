# Fadwa — Session 5 — 15 May 2026

## Summary

Three weeks since Session 4. Fadwa arrived clearly more grounded — better mental space, language shifting, habits held without shame. The creative/business block from last session took centre stage: the path to content creation, photography, and putting herself out there has been coded as dangerous (through inherited religious beliefs, family image pressure, and parental approval wiring), so the brain keeps finding exits before she even starts. Reframe offered: stop fighting the path, define a new one. The session introduced the Devotion framework — renaming the work as devotion to remove resistance — and located excitement as the entry point and fuel source. Fadwa identified holistic health, body, plants, and natural medicine as her genuine electric thread.

## Key Discussion

### Progress Check — What's Changed

Three weeks on: visualisation practice embedded (morning and evening, and she believes it now — not performing it). Language shifted day-to-day — more deliberate, less negatively loaded. Habits held without self-punishment when broken (e.g. Liverpool trip — she took parts of her routine she could carry, didn't spiral when the rest slipped). Identity starting to move: she declined Airbnb work in her head because "in 2–3 weeks I'll be making money the way I want to" — didn't say it aloud but felt it. Sent a cover letter to a photographer contact by acting faster than her doubt could catch up.

### The Block — What It Actually Is

Not knowledge or capability. Fadwa can describe exactly what she needs to do and how to do it. The block fires before any attempt — a series of escalating excuses (Liverpool trip, then menstruation, waiting for the next excuse) until action becomes impossible. The email to the photographer: started writing it, told herself she couldn't send it without a portfolio, removed it from her reminders entirely. She knows she could just send an honest email. She didn't.

Identified as a path problem: the route to creative work has been coded as threatening. The brain is doing its job, routing to safety. The work is not to push harder through that path — it's to route around it to the same destination.

### The Word "Work" and the Devotion Reframe

"Work," "career," "content creation" have accumulated so much baggage — self-sabotage loop, shame, the gap between what she's doing and what she feels she should — that they now route to feeling bad, not to the goal. Dan shared his own shift from "work" → "devotion" (~3 years ago): coupling actions to life purpose rather than obligation changed the energy entirely. Fadwa already does a version of this instinctively — she renamed "studying" to "mental stimulation" or "research" in her calendar to remove the resistance. The task is to extend that same move to the creative/career domain.

The Devotion structure: a big header — a purpose-level commitment tied to what genuinely matters (life's work, the relationship, family, spiritual expression) — under which anything important can be filed. Once something falls under the devotion header, the energy shifts from obligation to expression.

### Finding the Excitement First

Brain dump exercise: write everything — all avenues, all to-dos — not to bind yourself to the list, but to see where the electricity lives. Separate the exciting items. Start there every day. Excitement creates momentum, momentum carries into less energising work. The current pattern (clear obligations first, get to excitement as reward) depletes before you reach what energises you.

Fadwa's electric thread, identified live in session: holistic health, the human body, plants, natural medicine. Immediate, certain, zero hesitation.

### The Religious Belief File

A subconscious inherited belief surfaced: that being creatively visible — content creation, photography, putting herself online — might not be spiritually acceptable. Not a belief she consciously holds now; one that was programmed early and never explicitly updated. The brain, accepting it as gospel, has been routing away from creative work as a survival decision — prioritising eternal outcome over present frustration. She's moved past this intellectually (has had the conversation with her dad, understands her religion differently in the last 3–4 years) but the belief was never named or updated through direct experience. It was running silently underneath everything.

### Family Image and Parental Approval Wiring

Second layer: carrying the family's public image, and the way parental praise hardwires behaviour. Parents praised "smart" and "professional" — office job, suits, lawyer track. Anything outside that frame triggers a subconscious fear of losing that approval. The "slaying the giants" concept introduced: the moment when parents must be levelled — seen as people, not gods — so their worldview is no longer automatically inherited as your own. Fadwa hasn't made that move yet in this domain.

### How Beliefs Update

Beliefs don't update through argument. They update through experience that generates *knowing*, and the knowing overrides the belief. She didn't overcome the resistance to sending the job cover letter by reasoning through it — she acted faster than the doubt could catch up, felt the result, and that experience began the rewiring. The task: intentionally create more of those experiences on the new path. The feeling does the updating; the argument just prepares the ground.

## Content & IP

### Post Material
- Most people think the block is a motivation or discipline problem. It's a path problem — the route has been coded as dangerous, so the brain keeps you out. Stop pushing through the path. Find a new one to the same place.
- The word you use to describe what you do changes whether you do it. "Work" and "devotion" point to the same actions but produce completely different nervous system responses.
- We can get addicted to cortisol — the stress chemical — without realising it. Self-sabotage isn't failure; it's your brain routing back to a familiar state. That's not a weakness. It's wiring.
- Analogy that landed: "You're trying to get through the block. I'm saying walk around the corner — you'll get to the same place, just from a different direction."
- The child who can't stop playing: curiosity and excitement carry children effortlessly for hours. Adulthood doesn't kill that energy — it buries it under "should" and "must." It's still there. You have to start with it, not reward yourself with it.

### Frameworks & Concepts
- **The Devotion Reframe** — replacing "work" with a personally-defined concept of devotion that ties daily actions to life purpose; removes resistance by changing the meaning, not the action
- **Start With Excitement** — using genuine excitement as the entry point each day; fills the cup first, creates momentum that carries into less energising tasks; counters the "clear the hard stuff first" conditioning
- **New Path, Same Destination** — when a path to a goal has been coded as dangerous or shameful, the coaching move is to route around it rather than push through it; find a different route to the same place
- **Belief vs. Knowing** — beliefs update through experience that generates knowing, not through argument; the knowing overrides the belief; intentional new experiences are the mechanism for rewiring

### Coaching & Advisory Patterns
- When a client has full knowledge of what to do but zero movement, the block is not cognitive — it's in the meaning layer. What does this path *mean* to them, and what state does it route to? That's where to look.
- The religious/cultural layer can be invisible even to the client. Naming it precisely — not as a problem but as a belief file that's running — was enough to shift something. The question isn't "is this true?" but "is this belief still the one you want running the show?"
- Parental approval wiring: what your parents praised you for creates a hidden driver. Worth surfacing with any client stuck between their genuine direction and an inherited one.
- Fadwa's pattern: she acts well when she acts faster than her doubt. The cover letter, the job enquiry — both worked because she moved before the resistance could catch up. This is her edge. Build from it.
