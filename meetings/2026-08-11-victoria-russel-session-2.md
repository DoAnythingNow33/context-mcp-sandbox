## Key Discussion

### Tom (Sleevenote) passed on the festival
Victoria's read from an hour-long call with Tom: he rejected the opportunity partly on artistic taste (didn't like the accompanying art/soundscape) rather than commercial judgment, and cited no hardware left after the weekend. Dan's take: Tom is applying an "artist mindset" to what needs to be a product/business decision — comparing it to a past experience with his ex-boss Adam at Theme (the recording studio venture), where ego and control-taste got in the way of a real opportunity (an artist, Kendressa, who has since gone on to work with Drake) and ultimately cost Dan that working relationship.

### Sleevenote's misalignment and gaps
Tom told Victoria the team needs startup funding; separately, when Dan asked CY (Sleevenote's executive producer / likely angel investor) the same question, CY said they don't need investment, they need market fit — a direct contradiction. Dan's broader assessment: two founders, 12 years working together, no clear go-to-market plan, resistant to hardware/market research Dan has been sending them (e.g. Little Guy Net, a Gen Z wearable hardware brand with a strong social following Dan flagged as a comp — Tom's response was dismissive). Dan reads this as a sign the founders aren't serious about growing past their current size.

### Backup device partner secured
Victoria has independently reached out to another hardware brand (MP3-player-style wearable, Tamagotchi/Game Boy-esque, believed Singaporean or Chinese) as a fallback audio partner for the festival — they responded same-day, enthusiastic, and offered to supply three devices. Not the preferred British-brand fit, but solves the immediate need if Sleevenote doesn't come through. Dan also reiterated he can still lend his own device regardless of which partner is used.

### Dan and Victoria agree to work together
Victoria explicitly proposed regular check-ins, citing complementary skill sets — Dan's pattern-recognition for hardware/consumer-tech trends plus his read on founder psychology and behavior, her commercial-creative and business-development background. Early business model floated: an introduction/consultancy structure — connecting Dan and Victoria's networks (e.g. her contact from Native Instruments/Plugin Alliance, strong on GTM, already positive on Sleevenote) to opportunities in exchange for a fee/percentage, self-funding a longer-term goal of angel-investing in creative, multidisciplinary hardware/consumer startups. Victoria linked this to her existing "Mycelium" concept — private investors backing creative ideas across disciplines, with some ventures (the network/connector layer) funding the riskier, higher-upside bets.

### Personal / tangential
Extended tangent on trading cards (Dan's new hobby — Pokémon, Magic, football pack-ripping/reselling) and the psychology of collectible-value inflation (including its use as a money-laundering channel out of China), which fed directly into the founder-mindset discussion around perceived value, scarcity, and market fit.
