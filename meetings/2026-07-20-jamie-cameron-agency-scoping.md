# Jamie Cameron — Agency Scoping — 20 July 2026

## Summary

Shift in the relationship: this call reframes Jamie from coaching client to co-builder. Jamie wants to define what he and Dan can productize together and start pitching it — building AI agents/assistants as a service. Dan pitched his Hermes/context-folder AI assistant system as the core offering (positioned as either a standalone assistant for founders who can't afford a £50k/year hire, or a bridge between a founder and existing assistant). ## Key Discussion

### Productizing agents — the framing
Jamie opened the call wanting to move from ad hoc conversation to a working document covering everything they could productize and pitch — starting with AI agents. Dan flagged the risk early: agents need to be built on something one of them knows deeply, or built custom per client, which is a lot of infrastructure to build if it doesn't solve the right problem.

### Dan's pitch: the AI assistant / context-folder system
Dan walked Jamie through his own setup — a context folder (Obsidian-viewed) plus Hermes, an always-on agent running on a ~£7/month cloud VM, reachable any time via voice note. Structure: calendar, self, entities (people + projects), all cross-linked. Positioned two ways: (1) for founders without an assistant — a £500/month retainer covering setup and always-on availability, cheaper than a £50k/year hire; (2) for founders who already have an assistant — a bridge that takes 2am/weekend messages off both the assistant and the founder, with the assistant also given access to it.

### Cape Creative's Drape / Get Wrapped tool
Jamie demoed Cape Creative's internal vibe-coded tools: Drape (script/pitch writing → doc output) feeding into Get Wrapped (doc → formatted deck with video backgrounds from YouTube/Vimeo/MP4 uploads). Built by their founder Jess in a couple of weeks, in use ~6–8 weeks, well received internally, client-facing. Neither of them knows the exact model/cost stack behind it. Context: Cape Creative's European experiential team is being cut — hasn't produced revenue this year, business development has been difficult given the overhead of pitching against bigger agencies. Jamie is behind on landing a pitch that would let him stay with the company if it lands.

### Landing on the MVP: Notion + AI assistant
Dan named the sharpest angle: an AI assistant + Notion manager. Teams using Notion have a recurring pain — someone has to maintain it, and that's either a dedicated cost or a distraction from someone else's real job. Dan sees this as the concrete need-driven MVP (vs. "an AI system is cool" without an attached need). Jamie agreed and scoped it out: define why businesses use Notion, what inputs they'd feed in, what the AI system would actually do, and the roadmap. Timeline: build toward a working deck/demo over roughly the next 6 weeks (acknowledged as ambitious given Dan's current workload), aiming to start outbound around September.

### Pitch deck approach
Dan referenced a pitch deck he already built (reused a saved style/template) that got him a free product from a small team he approached at an event. Proposed reusing that approach for the Jamie/Dan pitch: position as "small businesses now have all the resources of enterprises, at a fraction of the cost" — covering sales enablement, marketing/content support, finance (e.g. "best CFO"), framed around "where are you plugging AI in now?" across verticals. Not meant to be Dan working solo — a joint pitch for both of them as trusted AI partners.

### Social Trait / AI pods workshop
Jamie has a 3pm call with Social Trait the same day and will raise the AI pods workshop idea again — it was floated once, both sides liked it, then went quiet. Not off the table, just deprioritized during a busy 6 weeks (Jamie was in Amsterdam, admin-heavy work). No major update to report otherwise.

### Domain / email
Dan offered Jamie a @doanythingnow email since Dan owns the domain. Jamie declined for now — not needed until they're further along; Jamie framed the current phase as "drawing the map, not stepping into it" — brainstorming and detailing before building.
