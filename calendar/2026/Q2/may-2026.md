## Patterns and Themes

- **Completion unlocks momentum.** Every big win — Skool, Growify scope, personal ontology — came from finishing something that had been building. The bottleneck pattern is real: one thing done opens several things downstream.
- **Cold outreach keeps getting displaced.** It's the one thing that directly builds pipeline, and it moved the least. Every week it was deferred by something that felt more urgent or more exciting. The 100x100 challenge is the structural answer — but it didn't actually start in May.
- **Breadth before depth.** Active threads multiplied (coaching, ontology, web dev, visual command centre, Skool, content) without any of them hitting consistent revenue. June needs to narrow and convert, not add.
