## From May — What We Learned and How We're Applying It

### May in one paragraph

May was a 3x month (£400 April → £1,230 May) built entirely on infrastructure and delivery. No cold outreach pipeline. The Skool community went fully live, Growify was signed and invoiced, the website launched, the personal ontology was built, and a new web dev client converted from cold. Everything that was supposed to enable £5k/month is now in place. May didn't generate £5k — it built the thing that will.

### Wins

- Skool fully live — all modules filmed, edited, posted; offer reshaped to free community with 1:1 upsell funnel
- Growify locked — first paying ontology/AI client; 50% advance invoiced; all 6 Phase 1 sessions scheduled
- Website live
- Personal ontology built — methodology foundation locked before Growify delivery began
- Shereen / Bubble Balloon Heaven converted — web dev client from cold call; signals offer is viable
- Jamie 4-session deal closed; his company now wants a team AI session
- Revenue 3x'd from month one — direction confirmed

### Lessons

1. **Completing one bottleneck unlocks multiple things downstream.** Skool done → outreach opens → content engine runs → client work can start. The sequencing was right; now that Skool is done, outreach is the next bottleneck to clear.
2. **Cold outreach without a pre-built list doesn't happen.** The 100x100 challenge was named and deferred five weeks in a row. The friction was never motivation — it was the decision cost of "who do I contact today." Engine with pre-built list removes that entirely.
3. **The scarcity belief was silently killing pipeline.** Operating from "clients are finite" turns outreach into gambling. The shift: the market for clarity and context is effectively infinite. Everyone needs it. Outreach becomes selection, not desperation.
4. **Reactive wins (new clients, new threads) are good — but they can't keep replacing proactive outreach.** Shereen, Jamie's company, Growify Phase 2 all emerged reactively. That pipeline will dry up without cold flow feeding it.

### How we're applying it in June

- **Outreach engine fixed and running this week** — list pre-built, no daily decision cost, just open and send
- **Deliver first, scope second** — Growify Phase 1 done by 21 Jun; Phase 2 scoped off the back of what we learn in sessions
- **Jamie company deal as the June anchor close** — one clean proposal, one meeting, close it
- **Skool group call 7 Jun** — first activation of the free community; treat it as a live conversion event
- **Content: 3 pillars per week** — 1 TM episode, 1 CWD episode, 1 green screen / viral clip; no perfectionism, just volume
