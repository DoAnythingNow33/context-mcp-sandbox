# AI Workflows

## What It Is

The full-stack offer. Helps businesses and individuals get the most out of AI — not just tool adoption, but a complete context architecture so AI agents have what they need to actually be useful. Combines coaching (the human), ontology mapping (the business), and context system build (the AI layer).

This is the premium tier — it includes everything else DAN does, applied to the AI adoption problem.

## Who It's For

- Businesses that want to adopt AI meaningfully but need their organisation's context made legible to AI
- Founders who want AI to function as a genuine thinking partner, not a prompt responder
- Agencies and service businesses where AI could automate or augment workflows but the context layer is missing

**Live example:** Growify — Shopify agency, founders-first ontology pilot agreed April 26.

## The Method

1. **Coaching layer** — the human doing the work needs their own ontology clear first
2. **Ontology mapping** — map the business so there's a defined, queryable context structure
3. **Context system build** — create the file architecture, agent prompts, and operational system
4. **Workflow integration** — identify which workflows benefit from AI augmentation and build the processes

The result is what DAN has built for itself — a living context system that any agent can work from intelligently.

## Key Selling Points

- **Ownership framing:** "Your context lives on your desktop, not inside Claude's servers"
- **Discrepancy detection:** AI flags gaps between stated vision and actual activity automatically
- **Palantir analogy:** What defense contractors pay millions for, available at agency scale
- **Bottom-up adoption:** Start with the people doing the work, not the founders' assumptions

**The mise en place analogy:** Sessions are prep work. Once prep is done, execution accelerates. Most AI use is the inverse — jumping straight to execution with no structure.
