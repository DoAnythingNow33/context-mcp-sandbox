# 1:1 Coaching — Timeline Shifting Therapy

## What It Is

1:1 coaching rooted in ontology, psychology and embodiment. The premise: every person is running internal software — beliefs, interpretations, survival patterns — that determines what timelines feel possible to them. When that software updates, reality shifts. Not metaphor. Mechanism.

This is not mindset coaching or motivation. It works at the level of the operating system.

## Who It's For

People who want to change their reality and are ready to do the inner work. They've tried conventional approaches — therapy, self-help, goal-setting — and found the ceiling. The change they want requires updating something deeper than behaviour.

## The Method

1. Ontological mapping — where the client currently is
2. Identification of belief structures and interpretive frameworks limiting perceived options
3. Psychological tools to process and release what's holding the current pattern
4. Embodiment work to ground the new operating state in the body, not just the mind
5. Follow-through structure so the shift doesn't revert

## Proof of Concept

Fadwa (Phase 1 complete): 4-session arc. Achieved ventral vagal state via imagination mapping + redefinition tools. Key tools confirmed: capturing wins, nighttime imagination protocol, arc creation. The Fadwa process is now the repeatable blueprint for future coaching clients and the foundation of the Skool curriculum.
