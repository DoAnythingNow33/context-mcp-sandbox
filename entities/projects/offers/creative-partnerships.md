# Creative Partnerships

## What It Is

DAN partners with creative talent — founders, artists, experts — and provides the organisational layer they don't have: communication, project management, systems, delivery structure. The creative partner brings knowledge, expertise, and creative vision. DAN handles everything else. 50/50 split on revenue or equity.

The model is built on a simple insight: most creative talent is held back not by lack of ability but by lack of structure.

## Who It's For

- Creative founders with a vision but weak on execution and organisation
- Experts who want to build a consulting or knowledge business but don't want to manage the business
- Talent who have something valuable and need a delivery partner, not an employee or agency

## The Method

DAN maps the creative partner's offer and vision (ontology mapping), builds the communication and delivery structure, manages projects and client relationships, and creates the systems for the partnership to operate. The creative partner delivers the core product or service. DAN delivers everything around it.

Agreement structure: 50/50 on revenue generated through the partnership. Equity where appropriate.

## Current Status

## Revenue Model

- High upside (equity) but revenue is deferred and unstructured
- 50/50 split sounds clean but partnership terms vary widely — needs a standard agreement template
- Creative partnerships generate meaning and alignment with arc, but may distract from faster-revenue pillars if not scoped well
