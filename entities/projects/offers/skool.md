# Skool Community

## What It Is

A Skool-based community for people who want to change their reality and need tools, support, and a peer group to do it. The community version of the 1:1 work — same methodology (ontology, psychology, embodiment) delivered at scale, with structured learning, live sessions, and community accountability.

## Who It's For

People earlier in the journey — not ready for or can't access 1:1 coaching, but know they need to change something fundamental. They want community and a structured path, not just content.

## Curriculum Foundation

Built directly from the Fadwa coaching arc (Phase 1 complete). The 4-session coaching process became:
- A repeatable blueprint for future coaching clients
- The foundation for the Skool classroom curriculum
- A proof-of-concept that the methodology works at depth

Key tools: capturing wins, nighttime imagination protocol, arc creation, imagination mapping, ventral vagal state work.
