# Ontology Building — Founder & Business Clarity

## What It Is

A structured process for founders and creatives with businesses that work but have hit a ceiling. We map their entities — the things their business is embedded in and made of — define them clearly, and make strategic decisions: what needs to be cut, what needs focused energy, what the business actually is underneath the noise.

For people who have tried conventional scaling methods and keep hitting the same wall. The ceiling is ontological. The tools need to match.

## Who It's For

Founders and creatives who:
- Have a business that works but isn't scaling the way they want
- Feel like they've tried the conventional routes and keep hitting the same wall
- Sense the blocker is internal — their relationship to the business, their vision clarity, their operating structure — not just tactics
- Want a structured methodology rather than another generic business coach

## The Method

1. **Entity mapping** — identify every significant entity the business is made of or embedded in (vision, team, clients, offers, brand, operations, culture)
2. **Definition** — articulate what each entity actually is, what it's for, and what it currently demands
3. **Strategic triage** — what serves the vision, what creates drag, what needs cutting, what needs focused attention
4. **Implementation structure** — decisions made, priorities set, action sequenced

Output: a functioning context architecture for the business — not a slide deck, but a living system the founder can use and update.
