# DoAnythingNow — Business Snapshot

## What It Is

Do Anything Now (DAN) is a coaching, consulting and project management business rooted in ontology, psychology and embodiment. The premise: people can change their reality when they update the internal software running their perception of what's possible. DAN works with individuals who want to shift timelines, founders who want to scale past the ceiling conventional methods haven't broken through, and businesses adopting AI.

Five offer lines. One brand. The through-line is the methodology: ontological mapping, psychological tools, embodiment practice, and structured systems.
