# Growify

## Company Context

- ~110 people, Shopify-focused e-commerce marketing agency
- New org structure just rolled out: team leads are now client-facing, senior brand managers moved into CRM/strategy roles — KRAs still settling
- Building Lookify (internal proprietary tool) as a parallel workstream
- Long-term goals: funding/exit readiness — AI embedded as a core differentiator

## Phase 1 — Agreed Scope

**Sessions:** 4 x 2-hour ontology sessions — Rishabh, Disha, Chandika, one more (Rohit mentioned but may not have time)

**Format:** One-on-one. Pen and paper to start. Context extraction, not training.

**Build:** ~1 week after sessions to construct the ontology

**Deliverable:**
- Visual map (Whimsical) showing how the business operates — current state vs. desired state, gaps visible
- Obsidian folder with deep structured context per person and for the company as an entity
- One group session to set up their Claude Code folders and show them how to use the system

**Extension path:** Expand to other pods/departments → build workflows per role → develop proprietary tools for their Shopify/luxury client use cases.

## Pricing

- Original proposal: ~£750
- Scope expanded (4 people vs. original)
- Daniel offered to stay at original budget to get started — relationship over margin at this stage
- Rishabh to come back with a number

## What Landed in the Call (Key Resonance Points)

- **Ownership framing:** "Your context lives on your desktop, not inside Claude's servers"
- **Discrepancy detection:** AI flags gaps between stated vision and actual activity automatically
- **Palantir analogy:** What defense contractors pay millions for, available at agency scale
- **Bottom-up adoption:** Start with the people doing the work, not the founders' assumptions
- **Disha's pushback (validate before scaling):** Don't involve the whole team until founders have 70–80% clarity
