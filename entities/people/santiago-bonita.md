# Santiago / Bonita

## Who Is Santiago

London-based Latin music producer, cultural programmer, and curator. Deep connections in Colombia — particularly Picotero culture. Track record: produced for / worked with Joe Bacan, La Muchacha, Fruco; curated London Latin Jazz Festival 2025; collaborated with South Bank; performed at the Barbican.

Cultural credibility is established. Current phase: building the business infrastructure and architecture around that credibility.

## What Is Bonita

Bonita is Santiago's creative brand and organisational vehicle.

| Arm | Person | Focus |

| Director / Creative | Santiago | Cultural vision, creative authority, all projects |
| CIC / Community | Oriana | Community programming, grant applications |
| Consulting | DAN | Brand partnerships, concept pitching, comms, delivery |

## Business Model

The longer arc: Bonita as the authority in Latin subculture heritage. Build the digital archive and grow an audience that cares about Latin cultural history. Drive that audience toward IP — books, vinyl, documentaries, films, series. Find Latin investors who want to tell these stories.
