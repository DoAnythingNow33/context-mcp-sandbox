# AI Workflows Outreach Playbook

## The Funnel

**AI Clarity Call (free, 30 mins)** → **Context Folder Build (£500, 1 day)** → ongoing workflows, projects, referrals

The audit is the entry point. It's genuinely useful to them even if they don't buy — that's what makes it shareable.

---

## The Offer

### AI Clarity Call (free)
- 30 minutes
- Honest audit of how they're using AI, where the gaps are, what's possible
- No pitch — just useful
- Natural close: "The next step, if it makes sense, is a Context Folder Build"

### Context Folder Build (£500, 1 day)
- Build the foundation that makes all their AI interactions coherent
- One day of focused work, delivered as a structured context system
- Everything after this (custom workflows, projects, referrals) flows from here

---

## Target Profile

**Who:**
- Founders, MDs, or Creative Directors at agencies, production companies, studios, consultancies
- 10–100 employees, or solo founders with clear 6-7 figure revenue signals (team, office, active client roster)
- Already using AI but chaotically — ChatGPT open in a tab, no system
- Already posting about AI, productivity, or scaling — open, just not organised

**Where:**
- UK-based first
- LinkedIn: search "Founder" + "Creative Director" + "Agency", UK, 11–50 employees
- People engaging with AI content but not selling it — they're buyers not competitors
- Second-degree connections from existing clients (Jamie, Growify, Santiago) — warm referral territory

**Pain they feel:**
Using AI randomly, not getting the return they should. The gap is always the same: lots of prompting, no system. Nothing that actually knows the business.

---

## LinkedIn — Two-Step Process

### Step 1: Connection Note (≤200 chars including their name)

Standard:
> Hi [Name] — I work with creative founders on AI systems. Saw your work at [Company], looks interesting. Would love to connect.

If they post about AI:
> Hi [Name] — saw your post on AI, resonated. I build AI systems for creative founders. Think we'd have a good conversation. Would love to connect.

### Step 2: Follow-up (send within 24hrs of accept)

> Hey [Name], thanks for connecting.
>
> Quick question — how are you actually using AI day to day? Not the theory, the reality.
>
> I ask because I work with founders like you to go from "prompting randomly" to having a system that actually knows your business. Usually a week's work, changes everything.
>
> I offer a free AI Clarity Call — 30 mins, honest audit, no pitch. Worth a conversation?

## Cold Email

>

>

>
> If it's useful, the next step is a Context Folder Build — I spend a day building the foundation that makes all your AI interactions 10x more effective. £500, one day, done.
>

>
> Dan

## Cold Call

Same target list. Find direct number via Companies House or LinkedIn.

**Opener:** "I work with creative founders on AI — do you have 2 minutes? I just want to ask you one question."

**The one question:** "How are you using AI day to day?"

That opens the audit conversation naturally.

---

## Weekly Target

**25 outreach attempts per week** (5 per day, Mon–Fri)
Mix of LinkedIn two-step, cold email, and cold calls.
At 1-in-3 conversion on audits → roughly 2 context builds per week → £1k baseline, before any project or referral work.
