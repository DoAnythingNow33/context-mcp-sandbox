# Website Rebrand: 3 Landing Pages

**Goal**: Move from a single website to 3 distinct landing pages, each with its own theme & conversion path, unified by a core "DoAnythingNow vibe."

## Current State

One website, live since 2026-06-02. Two-track business model (AI Workflows consulting + Reality Architects coaching) has created identity confusion → content stalling.

**Root problem**: The brand doesn't have a coherent visual + voice identity yet. It's functional but doesn't *feel* like DoAnythingNow.

## The Play: 3 Landing Pages

### Page 1: **AI Workflows** (Primary Income)
- Audience: Business leaders + ops teams needing AI integration
- Conversion: AI Clarity Call (free 30 mins) → Context Folder Build (£500/day)
- Theme: _Efficiency, clarity, systems_
- CTA: "Start with a clarity call"

### Page 2: **Reality Architects** (Long Game + Brand)
- Audience: Coaches, builders, creators seeking deeper transformation frameworks
- Conversion: Community (free) → 1:1 coaching
- Theme: _Depth, becoming, architecture of self_
- CTA: "Join the community" or "Explore 1:1 coaching"

### Page 3: **DoAnythingNow** (Hub)
- Audience: Anybody encountering DoAnythingNow for the first time
- Purpose: Clarify the unified identity across both tracks + point to the right page
- Theme: _Who is Dan, what's the philosophy, why both tracks exist together_
- CTA: "Explore" (routes to AI Workflows or Reality Architects)

## Design Brief: "Distill Your Vibe"

**What is the DoAnythingNow vibe?**

_This needs articulation before design._

Based on your work so far:
- **Operational clarity** + **philosophical depth**
- **Directness** without coldness
- **Systems thinking** applied to self, business, and others
- **Moving fast** but not rushing

**Design direction**: Dark-themed or minimalist? Warm or cool? What colours, typography, imagery?

**Action**: One sentence on DoAnythingNow's visual identity (before design starts).

## Workflow

1. **Identity distillation** — articulate the vibe in 1 sentence + visual direction
2. **Page structure** — outline content for each page
3. **Design** — create page templates (sketch or HTML mock)
4. **Copy** — write headlines, body, CTAs
5. **Handoff** — build or use platform (Webflow, Framer, etc.)

- Visual direction (you provide or we research together)
- Content outlines (you provide)
