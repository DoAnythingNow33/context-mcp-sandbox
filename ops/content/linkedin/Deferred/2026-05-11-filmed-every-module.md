I filmed every module in one day.

Three weeks of carry-forward — gone.

Every Sunday I'd write "edit modules" on the plan.
Every Friday I'd carry it to the next week.
I told myself I was making progress.

I wasn't making progress.
I was **managing** the bottleneck.

One day. Back to back. All of them.
When it was done, the shape of everything changed.
Clips can go out. Outreach can start. The next phase — unlocked.

Batching isn't efficiency.
It's how you break something instead of administrating it.

Most people keep chipping.
The move is to stop — and finish it.
