She sent the email before she could talk herself out of it.

That's the whole method.

She'd been trying to send it for three weeks.
Told herself she needed a portfolio first.
Removed it from her reminders.
Rewrote it four times.

Then one day she just sent it.

Not because the resistance disappeared.
Not because she finally felt ready.
Because she moved before the doubt could catch up.

**Speed isn't urgency. It's a bypass.**

Most people wait until they feel confident enough to act.

The confidence doesn't come first.
It comes after the send button.

You already know what you need to do.
Move before you can stop yourself.
