My son is 2.
Second baby arrives in August.
Fourteen weeks.

I'm building this on mornings.
6:30 to 11:30, Monday to Friday.
The rest of the day belongs to other things.

August doesn't negotiate.

Two under two.
The financial floor needs to be in place before then.
When I think about the runway as a number, it's a goal.
When I think about it in terms of **them** — it's something else entirely.

A real deadline isn't one you set.
It's one that's given to you.
By life. Not a calendar.

Most deadlines are soft.
Negotiable. Renewable.
The ones that aren't are where you find out what you're made of.
