You're not unmotivated. The route you've chosen has been coded as dangerous.

I see this with almost every client who knows exactly what they need to do and still can't do it.

They can describe the steps.
They understand the logic.
They've tried pushing through.

The brain isn't stopping them because they're lazy.

It's stopping them because somewhere along the way — through shame, through inherited belief, through something they were taught about what's acceptable — that path got filed as a **threat.**

And the brain's job is to keep you away from threats.

You're not fighting your motivation.
You're fighting a routing decision your nervous system made years ago.

Most people push harder through the same blocked path and call themselves failures when it doesn't work.

The move is to stop pushing.
Route around.

Same destination.
Different road.
