Your team didn't sign up for your personal development.

They're getting the results anyway.

When a leader does sustained inner work, it shows up in the room. Not as a speech or a new set of values on the wall. As presence. The meetings that don't spiral. The decision that comes from clarity instead of reaction.

The team didn't ask for this.

But they're operating in a different environment because of it.

**Inner work** is one of the highest-leverage investments a founder can make for their people. The industry just doesn't frame it that way.

Most founders invest in systems and talent. A few invest in themselves. The difference shows.
