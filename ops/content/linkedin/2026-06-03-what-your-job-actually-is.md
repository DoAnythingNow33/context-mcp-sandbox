AI isn't taking your job.

It's showing you what your job actually is.

For years, founders did robotic work by hand. Processing, formatting, scheduling, re-drafting. Not because it was yours to do. Because there was nothing else to do it.

That's changed.

When you hand the robotic work over, what remains is the work that actually matters. The relationships only you can hold. The judgment that requires your specific context. The move that can't be templated.

**That's** the job. AI just made it visible.

Most founders are anxious about being replaced.

The ones paying attention are getting more human at work than they've ever been.
