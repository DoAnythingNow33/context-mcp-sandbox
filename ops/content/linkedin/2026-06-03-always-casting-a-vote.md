There's no such thing as not deciding.

You're always casting a vote.

The call you haven't made. The price you haven't raised.

You've told yourself you're holding options open.

You're not. You're choosing.

Every week you defer, you cast a ballot for a specific timeline. Just not the one you say you want.

**Decisions** don't start when you make them. They start the moment you stop deferring.

Most founders wait until they feel ready.

Ready doesn't arrive.

The vote is happening right now, whether you show up to cast it or not.
