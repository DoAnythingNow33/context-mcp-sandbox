I stopped calling it work three years ago. Something shifted.

Not a rebranding exercise.
Not a mindset trick.

The word "work" had accumulated enough — obligation, anxiety, the gap between where I was and where I wanted to be — that every time I reached for it, it routed me somewhere bad.

So I named it something else.

**Devotion.**

Actions tied to what I'm actually building.
Not to a job. Not to a to-do list.

The actions didn't change.
The meaning did.

What you call something changes whether you do it.

Most people add discipline on top of a word they already hate and wonder why they keep running out of steam.

Rename the thing.

The resistance doesn't disappear — but it stops being the first thing you feel when you reach for the work.
