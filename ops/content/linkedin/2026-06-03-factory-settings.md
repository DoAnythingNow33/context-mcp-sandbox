You've designed your product down to the font.

Your life is still running on factory settings.

Most founders have taste. Clear opinions about UX, copy, positioning. They've built something with intention.

Then you look at how they actually live. Wake time inherited from old habit. Evenings formed around other people's routines.

Taste is the ability to recognise quality and shape it deliberately. Most people apply it to things they make or buy.

Very few apply it to the life they're actually living inside.

**Your life** has a texture. Most people let it happen to them.

A few decide to curate it.
