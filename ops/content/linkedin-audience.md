# LinkedIn Audience & Content Intent

## Who I'm writing for

Small and medium business owners who are actively trying to scale — not thinking about it, doing it. Founders. Decision-makers. People who move fast, hate being behind, and feel the weight of being the one who has to figure it all out.

They are:
- Running businesses with real revenue and real teams — past the "side hustle" phase
- Obsessed with leverage: they want to know what's working *right now*, not what worked in 2019
- Early adopters of tools, frameworks, and methods — they self-select into "I try things first"
- Hungry for decisions they can trust, not more information to wade through
- Quietly overwhelmed — they won't say it, but the gap between where they are and where they want to be is a constant pressure
- Suspicious of gurus and hype; they respond to people who are honest about the hard parts

They use LinkedIn to learn and to feel like they're not alone in what they're navigating.

---

## The emotional target

Every post should leave this reader feeling one of three things:

1. **Seen** — "He's describing exactly what I'm going through."
2. **Equipped** — "I didn't have a word for that before. Now I do."
3. **Confident** — "I'm not behind. I just needed to hear that."

The emotional move is: name their private experience before they've named it themselves. When they feel seen, they trust. When they trust, they lean in.

What to avoid:
- Making them feel judged for not knowing something
- Overwhelming them with frameworks when they're already overwhelmed
- Hype or inspiration that doesn't connect to a real decision they face

---

## The implicit promise every post makes

*I've navigated this. Here's what I found. You can use it.*

Not: "Here's my success story." Not: "Here's a tactic." The post positions Dan as someone in the arena with them — ahead by enough to be useful, close enough to understand the terrain they're actually on.

---

## Content lens: what cuts through for this audience

Apply this filter when generating post ideas:

| Post type | Why it lands |
|-----------|-------------|
| The hidden cost of a common habit | They're doing it too. Now they have to think. |
| The decision framework nobody names | Gives them a tool they can use tomorrow |
| "I got this wrong for years" | Honesty disarms. They trust faster after this. |
| The thing everyone's optimising for that doesn't matter | Challenges the default — they love a good contrarian hit |
| What scaling actually exposes | Names the private fear: *what if I'm not the right person to lead what I'm building?* |
| AI / new method applied to a real business problem | Practical and current — hits the "early adopter" nerve |

Posts should feel like a message from someone who has been in the room they're trying to get into.

---

## Tone calibration for this audience

- Direct. No hedging. They make decisions — write like someone who makes decisions.
- Credible through specificity, not credentials. Name the actual thing, not a general principle.
- Warm but not soft. They're under pressure. Match the register.
- Occasionally irreverent about sacred cows (hustle culture, vanity metrics, "just post consistently") — they're tired of the same LinkedIn noise.
