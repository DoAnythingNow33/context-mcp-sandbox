# Carousel Creation System

Guidelines, templates, and workflow for consistent carousel production.

## Core Principles

- **Opening slide**: Hook (question, stat, or promise)
- **Core slides**: One idea per slide, max 3–4 supporting points
- **Call to action**: Clear next step (link, DM, reply, or CTA)
- **Design consistency**: Theme, color, typography across series

## Series Templates

### Educational / Framework
- Slide 1: Question or insight
- Slides 2–4: 3 core ideas
- Slides 5–6: Example or case study
- Slide 7: Deeper reading / resource

### Narrative / Story
- Slide 1: The moment / setup
- Slides 2–5: Unfolding
- Slide 6: Insight / pivot
- Slide 7: What it means

### Announcement / Product
- Slide 1: What's new
- Slides 2–3: Why it matters
- Slides 4–5: How it works
- Slide 6: How to get it
- Slide 7: First 20 people [CTA]
